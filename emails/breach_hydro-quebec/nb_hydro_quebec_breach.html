SECURITY ALERT:

HYDRO-QUÉBEC CUSTOMER ACCOUNT INTRUSION

Hi {{contact.FIRSTNAME}},


We’re writing to make you aware of a security incident involving Hydro-Québec customer accounts. After detecting unusual login activity, Hydro-Québec has asked approximately 1.7 million customers to update their passwords.


If you have a Hydro-Québec online account, update your password as soon as you can. This email explains what happened, who needs to act, how criminals may try to take advantage of the situation, and how your Securizone subscription can help you stay on top of it.

What happened?

On October 20, Hydro-Québec’s cybersecurity monitoring detected unusual login activity, and roughly 2,500 customer accounts were accessed without authorization. According to Hydro-Québec, its internal systems were not compromised and no usernames or passwords were leaked from its systems. Instead, the intruder used email addresses and passwords that were already in use on other websites – a technique often called credential stuffing.

Who needs to act?

As a precaution, Hydro-Québec is asking customers whose online account (Customer Space) was created before 2023 to reset their password. If you have a Customer Space account, check whether you’ve received this request. If you don’t have one, there’s nothing to do with Hydro-Québec, but the same risk applies anywhere a password is reused.

What you can do now

We recommend the following steps:

  1. Update your Hydro-Québec password: If you have a Customer Space account, reset your password now and choose one you don’t use anywhere else. Also turn on two-step verification, which Hydro-Québec has now made available to all customers.

  2. Don’t reuse passwords: The intruder relied on email and password combinations already in use on other websites. Use a different password for each important account, such as email, banking and utilities, and consider a password manager to keep track of them. If you reused your Hydro-Québec password elsewhere, change it there too.

  3. Watch for Hydro-Québec impersonators: Scammers often pose as Hydro-Québec by text or email, using refunds, overdue bills or service cut-offs as bait. Hydro-Québec doesn’t issue refunds by text or email. If a message pushes you to act quickly, don’t click its links or call numbers it gives. Go directly to hydroquebec.com or the official app instead.

  4. Review your accounts and statements: Check your Hydro-Québec account for changes you didn’t make, review your bank and credit card statements for activity you don’t recognize, and check any alerts in your Securizone account.

  5. Share this with family and friends: Let the people close to you who have a Hydro-Québec account know, especially if they set it up years ago, since accounts created before 2023 are the ones being asked to reset.
